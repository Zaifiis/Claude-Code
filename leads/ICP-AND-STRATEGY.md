## The ICP

> **Owner-operated vacation-rental / villa management companies, 15–150 properties, in visually-driven leisure destinations, where the founder still personally approves marketing spend.**

### Why this profile and not another

1. **They already have your input material.** Every managed villa has a professional photo set. You need zero new assets — the job starts the moment they say yes.
2. **Their pain is recurring, not one-off.** Every new property onboarded needs marketing assets. That converts a one-time sale into a per-property retainer.
3. **The owner is the buyer.** At 15–150 properties there is no marketing department. The founder reads the WhatsApp and decides that day.
4. **$150 is below the thinking threshold.** For a company billing 20–30% on dozens of properties, $150 is not a capital decision. Ten videos at $1,500 is a marketing line item.
5. **They're measured on booking conversion.** Video's effect on listing conversion is the thing they already care about. You're not educating them on why video matters — only on why *your* video is cheap.

### Anti-ICP — do not waste outreach on these

| Segment | Why to skip |
|---|---|
| Solo Airbnb hosts (1–3 listings) | One-time $150 purchase. Acquisition cost exceeds lifetime value. They also have no company email — reaching them means scraping personal data, which is the wrong game to be in. |
| Vacasa, Sonder, Awaze, Evolve | Procurement, legal review, vendor onboarding, 6-month cycles. Your price point doesn't survive a vendor-onboarding form. |
| Long-term residential property managers | No marketing budget for video. Their listings rent themselves. |
| Hotels & resorts | Brand guidelines, agency of record, an existing content contract. Wrong door. |

## Market ranking — and the reasoning

I weighted markets on five axes: villa density, whether the owner is directly reachable, USD-equivalent budget, how content-driven bookings are, and timezone/channel friction from Pakistan.

### Tier 1 — Bali, Indonesia ⭐ start here

The single best market for you, and it isn't close.

- **Highest density of small owner-run villa management companies anywhere on earth.** Hundreds of firms in a 30km radius.
- **WhatsApp is the primary business channel.** Your message lands in a personal inbox, not a spam folder behind a filter. This is the entire ballgame — it removes the deliverability problem that kills cold email.
- **Bookings are Instagram-led.** Video is already understood as a revenue instrument, not a nice-to-have.
- **Priced in USD.** Villa nightly rates are quoted in dollars, so a $150 invoice needs no mental conversion.
- **Timezone**: UTC+8 vs Pakistan UTC+5 — a 3-hour gap. You can hold a live conversation.
- **English-speaking operators**, many expat-founded.

### Tier 2 — Dubai, UAE

- Enormous licensed holiday-homes sector, ferociously competitive on listing quality.
- AED is USD-pegged; marketing budgets are the highest in this tier.
- WhatsApp-first business culture, English-speaking, **1 hour ahead of Pakistan**.
- Operators publish mobile numbers openly — see leads 29–36.

### Tier 3 — Thailand (Phuket / Koh Samui)

Same dynamics as Bali with somewhat larger portfolios per firm. Inter Property Phuket alone manages 500+ properties.

### Tier 4 — US Sunbelt (30A/Destin, Smokies, Orlando, Joshua Tree)

- **Biggest absolute budgets** — this is where your $150 looks cheapest.
- But: email-first, more gatekeeping, more competition, and a 10–12 hour timezone gap.
- Treat as your **high-value tier, not your volume tier**. Notice that leads 38 and 39 publish *named individuals' work emails* — those are the two highest-quality direct lines in the whole list.

### Tier 5 — Europe / Australia

Real budgets, but GDPR (EU/UK) and the Spam Act (AU) mean cold email **must** carry sender identity and a working opt-out. Slower, more formal. Included selectively.

### Recommended split of your first 50 sends

```
Bali + Thailand + Dubai   ~60%   →  fast cash, WhatsApp, no deliverability risk
US                        ~30%   →  higher ticket, email, slower
Europe / Australia        ~10%   →  compliance overhead, best margins
```

## Where the leads came from, and what you must do before sending

Every contact detail in `leads-50.csv` is a **business** contact that the company publishes on its own website or public profile for the purpose of being contacted. No personal data of private individuals was collected.

**Important limitation, stated plainly:** the network policy on this machine blocked me from opening the company websites directly. Details were sourced from search-engine results, which are accurate but can lag site changes. The `contact_status` column tells you exactly what was published for each lead.

**Before your first send, do this** — it takes about 90 minutes for all 50:

1. Open each website; confirm the email/phone still matches.
2. For rows marked *"website only — pull contact from site"* (about 20 of them), grab the address from their Contact page.
3. Find the founder's name — check the About page, then LinkedIn. **A named greeting roughly doubles reply rates** over "Hi there".
4. Verify email deliverability (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce) before any bulk send. One bounce-heavy send can damage your sending domain permanently.
5. Send from a **separate domain** to your main one (e.g. `yourbrand.co` for outreach, `yourbrand.com` for real business). Warm it for 2–3 weeks. Cap at 30–40 emails/day per inbox.

## The pitch, by channel

### WhatsApp (Bali, Thailand, Dubai) — your primary channel

Keep it under 60 words. No attachments on message one — attachments trigger blocks.

> Hi [Name] — I saw [Villa Name] on your site, beautiful property.
>
> I turn villa photos into 30-second walkthrough videos for Airbnb and Instagram. No filming, no visit — I just use the photos you already have. Around $150 per villa.
>
> I made one from your listing photos so you can judge it. Want me to send it?

**Why this works:** it asks permission to send rather than dumping a link, which converts far better and won't get you reported. And it leads with proof, not claims.

### Email (US, Europe, Australia)

Subject: `[Villa Name] — 30-second walkthrough, no filming needed`

> Hi [Name],
>
> I made a short walkthrough video from the photos already on your [Villa Name] listing — no shoot, no site visit. It's 28 seconds: [link]
>
> If it's useful, I can do the rest of your portfolio at $150 per property.
>
> If not, tell me and I won't follow up.
>
> [Your name] — [company], [city]
> Reply STOP to opt out.

The last two lines are not optional in the EU/UK/AU. They also improve reply rates, because a visible exit signals you're a real business.

### The one thing that matters more than any of this

**Build the free sample before you send.** You have Runway and Kling and their photos are public on their own website. A 30-second video made from *their* villa converts at a completely different rate than any description of what you could do. Do this for the first 10 leads before touching the other 40.

At $150 a unit, your bottleneck is not lead volume — it's proof. Ten stunning samples will out-earn a thousand cold messages.

## Sequencing your first 90 days

| Phase | Days | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof** | 1–7 | Pick 10 Tier-A Bali leads. Build a free walkthrough for each from their public photos. |
| **First revenue** | 8–21 | Send all 10 via WhatsApp. Expect 3–5 replies, 1–2 paying. Price the first two at $99 to buy testimonials. |
| **Volume** | 22–45 | Work leads 1–36 (Bali/Thailand/Dubai). Now you have samples AND testimonials. |
| **Ticket size** | 46–75 | Move to US leads 37–48 with a portfolio behind you. Same product, quote $250–400 — the US market supports it. |
| **Retainers** | 76–90 | Go back to anyone who bought once and pitch per-property onboarding: *"every new villa you take on, we do the walkthrough — $150, 48-hour turnaround."* This is where the business actually is. |

**The strategic point:** don't stay at $150. Use the cheap price to buy proof in Bali, then charge US rates for identical work. The same 30-second video that's a $150 impulse buy in Canggu is a $400 line item in Destin.

## Compliance — the short version

- **UAE / Indonesia / Thailand**: WhatsApp business outreach is normal and accepted. Keep it personal and low-volume; don't blast.
- **US**: CAN-SPAM — B2B cold email is legal. You must include a real physical address and a working opt-out.
- **EU / UK**: GDPR + PECR — B2B email to a company address is permissible under legitimate interest, but you must identify yourself and honour opt-outs immediately.
- **Australia**: Spam Act — consent, identification, and unsubscribe are all mandatory.
- Never buy a scraped list. Never message solo hosts on personal numbers.
