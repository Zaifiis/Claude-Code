## Getting the rest

**What I could not do:** there is no Apollo extension, account or API key in
this environment, and the network policy blocks apollo.io along with every other
third-party site. That block is also why contact strings came from search rather
than from the companies' own pages.

**What works, in order of yield for this specific ICP:**

1. **Instagram DM — best channel for Bali and Thailand.** These firms live on
   Instagram; the founder usually runs the account personally. Higher answer
   rate than any email you will ever buy, and it costs nothing.
2. **The company's own About page.** Roughly a third of these firms name their
   team. Free, accurate, current.
3. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — filter: job titles *Founder, Co-Founder, Owner,
   Managing Director, General Manager*; industry *Hospitality / Real Estate*;
   headcount *11–200*; location *Bali, Phuket, Koh Samui, Dubai*. Strong for
   Dubai, weaker for Indonesia.
4. **Apollo / Hunter / Clearbit** — feed them the company domain plus the name
   from the table above. Apollo is genuinely good, but understand its bias
   before you pay: coverage is deep on US and European B2B and **thin on
   owner-run SE Asian hospitality**, which is exactly your best market. Expect
   good results for leads 29–50 and poor results for 1–28.
5. **WhatsApp Business profile.** Many Bali firms put the owner's name straight
   on the business profile. Message the published number and the name appears.

**The honest trade-off:** Apollo will get you US contacts efficiently and will
mostly fail on Bali. Instagram and WhatsApp will get you Bali contacts
efficiently and are useless for Florida. Use both, matched to the market — do
not pay for one tool and expect it to cover all fifty.

## One thing worth saying plainly

At $150 a video, a named contact is worth far less than a finished sample. A
message that opens *"I made a walkthrough from your Villa Ava photos, want to
see it?"* — sent to a generic `info@` — will beat a perfectly-enriched
founder's direct email carrying a generic pitch. Spend the next hour in Runway,
not in a data tool.
